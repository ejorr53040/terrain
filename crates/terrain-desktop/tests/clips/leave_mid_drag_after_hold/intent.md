# Hand leaves mid-drag, after a hold

Recorded as the "Hand leaves mid-drag" clip; kept because it's how a drag
comes naturally.

## Performed

- t ≈ 2.0 s  the hand has control, open
- t ≈ 3.1 s  pinch closes (thumb–index 2.5 cm)
- t 3.1–4.4  pinch held still (≈ 1.3 s)
- t 4.4–5.2  pinched hand moves right (palm x 0.56 → 0.66 of the view)
- t ≈ 5.3 s  hand leaves the view, and stays out

## Expected actions

- a left drag: Press Left where the pinch closed once it starts moving,
  then Release Left within about 0.3 s of the hand vanishing (a still
  pinch that then moves drags)
- in every case: no button held after the hand leaves

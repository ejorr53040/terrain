# Fist forming (no click)

## Performed

t 0–2  hands on the keyboard
t 2–3  raise your right hand open, palm to the camera, to chest height in
       front of the screen; hold it still for a full second
t 4–5  slowly curl the open hand into a fist (about a second)
t 5–6  hold the fist still
t 6–7  slowly open it again; hold open
t 8    lower the hand back to the keyboard and leave it there to the end

## Expected actions

- the hand takes control
- at most one window drag (Super + Left pressed, then released) with the
  pointer still
- never a left click or right click from the pinch the hand passes through
- control let go after the hand is lowered

# Drag after a hold

Recorded as the "Drag" clip; kept because it's how a drag comes naturally.

## Performed

- t ≈ 1.5 s  the hand has control, open
- t ≈ 2.4 s  pinch closes (thumb–index 2 cm)
- t 2.4–4.1  pinch held still (≈ 1.7 s)
- t 4.1–5.2  pinched hand moves right (palm x 0.57 → 0.69 of the view)
- t ≈ 5.8 s  hand opens, then is lowered

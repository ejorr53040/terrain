# Hand leaves mid-drag

## Performed

t 0–2  hands on the keyboard
t 2–3  raise your right hand open, palm to the camera, to chest height in
       front of the screen; hold it still for a full second
t 4    pinch and start moving right at once: a pinch held still for
       over half a second is a long press (right click)
t 4–7  keep pinching while dragging slowly to your right, on past the
       screen's edge, until the hand leaves the camera's view (slowly: a
       fast sweep blurs the pinch open, which ends the drag early)
t 7–10 keep it out of view, still pinched

## Expected actions

- the hand takes control
- Press Left at the pinch, the pointer moves right, then Release Left
  within about 0.3 s of the hand vanishing
- nothing left held; control let go

# Long press

## Performed

t 0–2  hands on the keyboard
t 2–3  raise your right hand open, palm to the camera, to chest height in
       front of the screen; hold it still for a full second
t 4    pinch, and hold the pinch perfectly still
t 4–6  keep holding still (2 s)
t 6    open the hand; hold it still for a second
t 7    lower the hand back to the keyboard and leave it there to the end

## Expected actions

- the hand takes control
- one right click (Press Right, Release Right) when the pinch opens, where
  the pinch closed (a pinch held still 0.6 s right-clicks on opening)
- no left click
- control let go after the hand is lowered

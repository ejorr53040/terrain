# Vee scroll sideways

## Performed

t 0–2  hands on the keyboard
t 2–3  raise your right hand open, palm to the camera, to chest height in
       front of the screen; hold it still for a full second
t 3    make a Vee: index and middle fingers up, the others curled
t 3–5  move the Vee about 10 cm right and hold it there
t 5–7  move it about 10 cm left of where it began and hold it there
t 7–8  bring it back to where the Vee began, then open the hand
t 8    lower the hand back to the keyboard and leave it there to the end

## Expected actions

- the hand takes control
- PENDING until Vee exists (#22): scrolls right, then left; stops when it
  returns; the pointer stays frozen; no buttons
- control let go after the hand is lowered

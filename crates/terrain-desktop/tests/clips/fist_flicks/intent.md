# Fist flicks (herdr panes)

## Performed

Before recording: focus a herdr terminal split into panes.
Flick the way you naturally would; don't slow down to be careful.
t 0–2  hands on the keyboard
t 2–3  raise your right hand open, palm to the camera, to chest height in
       front of the screen; hold it still for a full second
t 4    make a fist and flick it ~10 cm left, then open the hand
t 5.5  fist, flick right, open
t 7    fist, flick up, open
t 8.5  fist, flick down, open

## Expected actions

- the hand takes control
- four pane moves, in order: Ctrl+Alt+H, Ctrl+Alt+L, Ctrl+Alt+K, Ctrl+Alt+J
  (a flick counts if it covers 0.1 screen widths within 200 ms of the fist
  closing; a slower one becomes a window drag, which tells us to tune it)
- no left or right click
